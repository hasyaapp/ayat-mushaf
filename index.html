Tajwid Warna

A Canva app for tajwid lessons. Pick a surah and up to 20 verses, and every letter that follows a tajwid rule is colored. Color all rules or just one lesson, such as nun sakinah and tanwin, and add a legend that names each color. The verses use the Madinah mushaf script as crisp vector images. The Indonesian translation (Kemenag RI) and a surah reference are optional.

Previously named Ayat Mushaf.

Rules and colors

  • Ikhfa'
  • Idgham bighunnah
  • Idgham bilaghunnah
  • Iqlab
  • Ikhfa' syafawi
  • Idgham mimi
  • Idgham mutajanisain / mutaqaribain
  • Ghunnah (nun or mim with shaddah)
  • Qalqalah
  • Mad, 2 harakat (small alif, waw or ya)
  • Mad 'aridh lissukun (2, 4 or 6 harakat)
  • Mad wajib muttashil (4 or 5 harakat)
  • Mad jaiz munfashil (2, 4 or 5 harakat)
  • Mad lazim (6 harakat)
  • Not pronounced (hamzah washal, lam syamsiyah, silent alif, waw or ya)

Letters read clearly, such as izhar, keep the text color, as in most tajwid mushafs.
