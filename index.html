Ayat Mushaf

A Canva app that adds Quran verses in the Madinah mushaf script as crisp vector images, with an optional Indonesian translation (Kemenag RI) and a surah reference. Choose any surah and up to 20 verses, set the line width, alignment and colour, and see a preview before adding.
