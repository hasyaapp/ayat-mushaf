Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy covers the Ayat Mushaf app for Canva ("the app"), published by Aksara Nusantara.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no cookies of its own, and your choices are not sent to us.

Verse data

When you pick a surah, the app downloads that surah's verse data from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may record technical request data such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement. The request contains only the surah number.

Canva

The app runs inside Canva. Verses you add are uploaded to your Canva account as images and stored as part of your design, under Canva's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this changes, we will update this page before the new version of the app is released. Questions: asharyhsm@gmail.com.
