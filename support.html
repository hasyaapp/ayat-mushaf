Ayat Mushaf support

Found a mistake in a verse, a mark or a translation, or need help? Email asharyhsm@gmail.com. Please include the surah and verse numbers and, if possible, a screenshot.

Questions

  • Can I edit the Arabic text? The verses are added as a vector image so the mushaf script stays exact. You can resize it and change its colour from Canva's toolbar; the translation and reference are normal text you can edit.
  • How do I change the colour? Pick a colour in the app before adding, or select the verses in your design and use the colour button in Canva's toolbar.
  • Why at most 20 verses? Longer passages become very tall and too small to read on one page. Add them in parts, for example on separate pages.
  • When is the Basmalah added? Only when you start from verse 1 of a surah other than Al-Fatihah (where it is verse 1) and At-Taubah (which has none). You can turn it off.
  • Which translation is used? The Indonesian translation by the Ministry of Religious Affairs (Kemenag RI), as published by the Quranic Universal Library.
  • Does it work offline? No. Verse data is downloaded when you pick a surah.
